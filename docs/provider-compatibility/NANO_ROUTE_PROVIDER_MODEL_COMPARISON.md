## 2. Comparison Safety Invariants
- **No Subjective Benchmarks**: Does not present subjective "intelligence", "quality", or "leaderboard" rankings.
- **Authoritative Cost Only**: Pricing is derived strictly from Step 43/56 pricing catalogs without third-party web scraping.
- **Responsive Layout**: Tables scroll horizontally on narrow displays and high-DPI scaling factors without overflowing the dialog boundary.
